The Planetary Neighbor: K2-201

A three-page planetary science communication project designed as a vintage newspaper, combining scientific research, explanatory writing, and visual design to introduce the K2-201 planetary system to a general audience.

Planetary Science Adobe Photoshop Science Communication Visual Design

Overview

Project Overview

I created The Planetary Neighbor as a final project for my introductory planetary science course at the University of California, Riverside.

The project presents information about the K2-201 planetary system through the format of a fictional vintage newspaper. My goal was to communicate planetary science concepts in a way that was visually engaging while remaining understandable to readers without a technical background.

Scientific Content

Communicating Exoplanet Science

The publication covered several topics in exoplanet and planetary science, including planetary characteristics, orbital properties, transit detection, comparisons between planets in the system, and questions surrounding planetary habitability.

I organized the scientific information into articles, planetary profiles, data summaries, graphics, and explanatory sections so that each page could communicate both individual facts and larger scientific ideas.

Design

Visual Design Process

I designed the full three-page publication in Adobe Photoshop. This included page layout, typography, image placement, informational panels, graphics, article organization, and the overall visual hierarchy.

I chose a vintage newspaper aesthetic to give the project a distinct identity while still organizing a large amount of scientific information into a structured and readable format.

Final Project

The Three-Page Publication
